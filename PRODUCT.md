# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cross Multiply is for people taking quick casual breaks and for puzzle enthusiasts who enjoy compact logic challenges. Players are likely on mobile or a nearby desktop, looking for a short session that rewards focus without demanding a large time commitment.

## Product Purpose

Cross Multiply is a multiply-first number puzzle with generated levels, difficulty progression, hints, hearts, and offline play. It exists to turn small moments into a cozy daily ritual: players should be able to open the game, solve a satisfying puzzle, make visible progress, and leave feeling sharper rather than drained.

## Brand Personality

Focused, tactile, playful. The product should feel thoughtful and responsive, with enough personality to make repeated play inviting. It should treat math as a satisfying puzzle material, not as homework.

## Anti-references

Avoid school worksheet energy, casino or mobile-game grind patterns, sterile math-tool minimalism, neon arcade styling, and corporate SaaS conventions. Progression should feel motivating without becoming manipulative, and the interface should not bury the puzzle under promotional chrome.

## Design Principles

- Keep the puzzle first: every surface should make the board, targets, current mode, and next useful action easy to understand.
- Make each tap feel intentional: interactions should be tactile, legible, and forgiving on mobile screens.
- Reward quiet mastery: feedback should acknowledge progress without turning the experience into a noisy reward loop.
- Preserve daily comfort: sessions should feel approachable, warm, and repeatable across light and dark contexts.
- Teach through play: modifiers, hints, and difficulty changes should reveal themselves through clear state changes and concise copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target has been specified yet. The product should still work well on mobile, with strong touch targets, responsive layouts, readable text, non-color-only puzzle states, and reduced-motion-friendly feedback where motion is used.
